Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, iOS version and what you were doing. Do not post anything private.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes your account and all data linked to it.

The app says it can't reach the server. Check your connection. Changes made offline sync when you are back online.

Who can see my characters? You, and members of campaigns you share them with.
