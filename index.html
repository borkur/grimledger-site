Mörk Borg Tracker

An unofficial character tracker for the MÖRK BORG tabletop game, for iPhone and iPad.

What it does

  • Track HP, omens, stats and inventory with carrying capacity.
  • Roll tests against a DR with clear results.
  • Run a campaign as GM: party overview, shared roll feed, misery counter and event log.
  • Sync across devices. Works offline and catches up when you are back online.
  • Sign in with email or Sign in with Apple. Delete your account any time from Settings.

No ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy. Need help? Visit support.
